04 / MOSTLY DECENT PERSON

Hi, I'm Mario.

I started my career in game development in 1998 as an artist and moved into game design in 2004. Since then, I've worked across level design, mission design, gameplay, and storytelling on a wide range of games.

I'm also a UCLA-trained feature film writer and the author of an award-winning short screenplay. What connects both sides of my work is a fascination with how stories become experiences.
